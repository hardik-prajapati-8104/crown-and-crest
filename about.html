About Crown & Crest

A licensed Dubai event management company delivering refined, perfectly run events.

Who We Are

A Dubai event company built on detail, trust and taste.

CROWN & CREST EVENT MANAGEMENT L.L.C is a Dubai-registered company, licensed by the Department of Economy & Tourism, specialising in the planning, production and management of events and live entertainment.

Our licence covers the full spectrum of event services: events organising and management, wedding parties, parties and entertainment, artistic talent contracting, performing arts and music festivals, live theatrical show production, laser and light shows, and picnic and camping organising. That breadth lets us deliver complete experiences rather than isolated services.

Based in Al Khabaisi, we serve clients across Dubai and the UAE, bringing international-standard planning with a genuine understanding of regional traditions and expectations.
