Planning Your Dream Wedding in Dubai: What to Decide First

Venue, season, guest list and vendors: the decisions that shape everything else about your wedding.

WeddingsCrown & Crest Editorial4 min read

Dubai is a world-class wedding destination, with beachfront ceremonies, ballrooms, gardens and desert settings. A few early decisions make the rest of the planning smoother.

Start with the guest list and budget

These two numbers determine venue size, catering style and entertainment. Agree on them before falling in love with a venue.

Choose the season wisely

Cooler months suit outdoor ceremonies and receptions; summer weddings work best in indoor venues. Season also affects venue availability and pricing.

Select the venue and date together

Popular venues book early. Have two or three alternative dates ready and visit shortlisted venues in person.

Define your style

Traditional, modern, intimate or spectacular: a clear style guides décor, florals, music and attire, and keeps the day coherent.

Book key vendors early

Photographers, entertainers, caterers and florists with strong reputations are often reserved many months ahead.

Plan multi-day celebrations

Mehndi, sangeet, engagement and reception events each need their own timeline. A planner can coordinate them as one story.

Confirm legal and documentation requirements

Marriage documentation depends on nationality and religion. Check current requirements with the relevant authorities early.

The best weddings feel personal. Every detail should say something about the couple.
