Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

What types of events do you manage?

We organise corporate events, weddings, private parties, music and performing arts festivals, theatrical shows, laser and light shows, and picnic and camping experiences, along with artistic talent contracting.

Are you a licensed company?

Yes. CROWN & CREST EVENT MANAGEMENT L.L.C holds Commercial Licence No. 1596598 issued by the Dubai Department of Economy & Tourism.

How far in advance should I contact you?

As early as possible. Weddings and large events benefit from several months of planning, but we also handle shorter timelines where suppliers and venues are available.

Do you offer full planning or only partial services?

Both. You can engage us for end-to-end planning, for specific elements such as entertainment or décor, or for day-of coordination.

How is pricing determined?

Every event is quoted individually based on scope, guest numbers, venue, suppliers and timing. We provide an itemised proposal so you can see exactly where the budget goes.

Can you work with my chosen venue or vendors?

Absolutely. We can work with your preferred venue and suppliers, or recommend trusted partners from our network.

Do you arrange permits and approvals?

We coordinate with venues and, where required, the relevant authorities for entertainment, music, lighting effects and outdoor set-ups. Requirements vary by event and location, and we advise on these during planning.

Do you organise events outside Dubai?

Yes. We serve clients across the UAE and can discuss destination events on request.

What are your payment terms?

A deposit secures your date, with the balance scheduled in stages as the event approaches. Full terms are set out in your proposal and in our Terms & Conditions.

How do I get started?

Send us an enquiry through our contact page, call +971 52 871 6837 or message us on WhatsApp. We will arrange a consultation to discuss your event.
