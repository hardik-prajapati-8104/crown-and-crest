Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

CROWN & CREST EVENT MANAGEMENT L.L.C ("we") is a Dubai-based event management company. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you use this website or contact us.

2. Information we collect

  • Details you provide: name, email, phone number, event type, date, guest numbers and your message.
  • Technical information such as browser type and pages visited, if analytics tools are enabled.
  • Information you share while planning an event with us.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare proposals.
  • To plan, deliver and manage events you engage us for.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

4. Sharing information

We share information with venues, suppliers and performers only as needed to deliver your event, and with professional advisers or authorities where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Enquiry form

Our enquiry form opens your email app or WhatsApp with your message prepared. Your details are transmitted through those services, subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies

This website uses only the minimum technical storage needed to function. If analytics or advertising tools are added in future, this policy will be updated.

7. Data security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information and retain it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us, subject to applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
