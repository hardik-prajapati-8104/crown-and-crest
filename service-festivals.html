Performing Arts & Music Festivals

Festivals are living, breathing productions. We plan and execute performing arts and music festivals with the operational discipline large crowds demand.

Our festival services cover programming, stage and site planning, artist coordination, technical production, ticketing support, crowd flow, safety planning and on-site operations.

We work with venues, sponsors and authorities, ensuring the creative vision is supported by robust logistics.

What's included

  • Festival concept, programming and line-up planning
  • Stage, sound, lighting and AV production
  • Site layout, crowd flow and facilities planning
  • Artist booking and backstage management
  • Sponsor and exhibitor activations
  • Ticketing and access-control coordination
  • Health, safety and permit coordination
  • Post-event reporting and debrief
