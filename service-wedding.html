Wedding Planning

Your wedding should feel effortless to you and unforgettable to your guests. We handle the planning so you can be fully present.

We design weddings and receptions of every scale, from intimate ceremonies to grand celebrations, drawing on your culture, taste and budget. Every element is coordinated: venue, styling, florals, catering, entertainment, photography liaison, guest flow and the timeline of the day.

A dedicated planner stays with you from the first consultation to the last dance, and a day-of team makes sure every moment lands as planned.

What's included

  • Full planning or partial / day-of coordination
  • Venue sourcing and site visits
  • Theme, décor, floral and lighting design
  • Catering, cake and beverage coordination
  • Entertainment, DJs, live music and performers
  • Guest list, invitations and RSVP management
  • Vendor management and payment scheduling
  • Mehndi, sangeet, engagement and reception events
